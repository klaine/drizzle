- **Two buffers** with a crossfade knob (e.g. a Scan tape and a Live ring, or a sample and a tape).

- **Grain envelope follower** as a matrix source (the cloud's own level driving the filter).
- **Spectral freeze** or FFT-based modes: deliberately out of v1 scope.
- **Shimmer** (pitched feedback) in the reverb.
